**Target Audience:**
Curious learners ages 18–45 who want to understand economics, trade, and global finance in plain language — not MBA students, but everyday people who want to make sense of the world around them

---

## 2. Tone of Voice

**Voice:** Friendly professor explaining complex ideas with everyday analogies — clear, conversational, never jargon-heavy, never condescending

**Sentence Style:** Short declarative sentences. Max 15 words per sentence in scripts. No passive voice. Lead with the concept, follow with the analogy.

**Analogy Style:** Everyday life — grocery stores, household budgets, local markets, traffic, weather. Never use finance jargon to explain finance concepts.

**Forbidden Words (never use):**
`simply`, `just`, `obviously`, `easy`, `basic`, `guaranteed`, `explosive`, `massive`

## 6. Animation Sequence (6 steps — always in this order)

1. `fadeIn` — element fades into view
2. `slideDown` — header slides down from top
3. `wordIn` — key terms appear word by word
4. `slideUp` — supporting text slides up
5. `countUp` — stats animate from 0 to value
6. `bounce` — CTA or key icon bounces once

**Transition:** slide-up
**Duration:** 400ms
**Easing:** ease-out

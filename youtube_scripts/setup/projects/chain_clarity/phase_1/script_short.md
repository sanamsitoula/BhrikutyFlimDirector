[RAPID CUT VISUALS: block diagram animated, hash demo, chain linking]

SPOKEN:
"A block is a sealed container. It holds three things.

ONE: the transaction data.
Who sent what to whom.

TWO: the timestamp.
The exact moment it was sealed.

THREE: the hash.
A digital fingerprint of everything inside.

Change one byte of that data — the fingerprint changes completely.

Here's the important part.
Every block also stores the fingerprint of the block before it.
Block 101 knows what block 100 looked like.
Block 100 knows what block 99 looked like.

If someone alters a block from six months ago — its fingerprint changes.
The next block no longer matches.
The one after that doesn't match either.
The chain breaks forward from the point of tampering.

And thousands of computers around the world — each holding their own copy — notice immediately."

# Music Brief — Phase 1
## Chain Clarity · "How Blockchain Works — No Bank Required"

**Brand sound identity:** Electronic ambient / lo-fi tech · 88–112 BPM · focused, intelligent, grounded
**Forbidden:** trap, hype EDM, aggressive dubstep, party anthems, countdown builds

---

## LONG-FORM VIDEO (12 min) — Section-by-Section

---

### INTRO / HOOK MUSIC (0:00–0:12)

**Energy level:** 8/10 — high tension, not hype
**BPM:** 105–110
**Mood:** Dramatic revelation. The weight before the explanation.
**Instrumentation:** Sparse piano or muted strings hit, deep sub-bass pulse, minimal synth texture
**Suggested dynamics:** Starts near-silent, swells at the 4-second mark under "That same week, Bitcoin..."
**Volume in mix:** 20–30% under voice — never compete with narration

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `cinematic tech tension lo-fi`
- `minimal electronic dramatic underscore`
- `ambient technology reveal`

**Specific track qualities to find:**
- Must have a natural swell point 3–5 seconds in
- No drums in the first 8 seconds
- No melody that will distract from the facts being stated

---

### SECTION 1 — THE TRUST PROBLEM (0:12–2:30)

**Energy level:** 5/10 — background presence only
**BPM:** 90–95
**Mood:** Curious, slightly tense, building understanding
**Instrumentation:** Minimal lo-fi beat, soft pad, subtle arpeggio
**Volume in mix:** 15–20% — pure background, never distracting

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `lo-fi study electronic minimal`
- `focused ambient tech background`
- `calm intelligent electronic`

**Transition cue:** Music should fade slightly when the Byzantine Generals analogy begins — let the storytelling breathe.

---

### SECTION 2 — WHAT IS A BLOCK? (2:30–5:00)

**Energy level:** 5/10 — steady focus energy
**BPM:** 92–98
**Mood:** Methodical. Explanatory. The classroom moment.
**Instrumentation:** Steady lo-fi beat, warm pad, occasional high-hat
**Volume in mix:** 15% — purely supportive

**Note for editor:** When "Third: the hash" is spoken, consider a micro-pause in the music or a brief filter sweep to signal this is the most important element of the section.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `lo-fi tech study beat`
- `ambient focus electronic 95 bpm`

---

### SECTION 3 — WHAT IS A HASH? (5:00–7:15)

**Energy level:** 6/10 — slight lift for the technical demo
**BPM:** 98–105
**Mood:** Technical curiosity. A discovery moment.
**Instrumentation:** Slightly more rhythmic. Clean kick pattern. Synth arpeggio that feels mathematical/precise.
**Volume in mix:** 15–20%

**Key moment:** When the two hash examples appear on-screen (SHA-256 of "Hello" vs "Hello!"), the music should feel like a small reveal — not a big dramatic sting, but a subtle change in the arpeggio or a small rise.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic focused discovery`
- `minimal tech arpeggio beat`
- `lo-fi electronic curious`

---

### SECTION 4 — THE CHAIN (7:15–9:00)

**Energy level:** 7/10 — building tension through the cascade description
**BPM:** 100–108
**Mood:** Escalating tension, chain reaction energy
**Instrumentation:** Layered bass, rhythmic synth, tension building through the cascade description
**Volume in mix:** 20–25% as the cascade ("block 501 invalid... block 502...") builds

**Key moment:** As the speaker describes the cascade of block invalidation, music should build noticeably — peaking right before "The past is locked." Then drop back to low volume for that line.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic tension build ambient`
- `cinematic tech escalation lo-fi`
- `electronic rising tension underscore`

---

### SECTION 5 — THE NETWORK (9:00–11:00)

**Energy level:** 6/10 — empowered and grounded
**BPM:** 98–105
**Mood:** Empowering. The scope of the network. Intelligent awe.
**Instrumentation:** Warm synth pads, steady beat, slight harmonic richness
**Volume in mix:** 15–20%

**Note:** This section should feel like "everything clicking into place." The music should be the most harmonically resolved of the video — the answer, not the question.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic ambient empowering`
- `lo-fi tech resolved warm`
- `intelligent electronic background`

---

### CTA (11:00–12:00)

**Energy level:** 7/10 — memorable and forward-looking
**BPM:** 105–110
**Mood:** Satisfying conclusion. Optimistic forward pull. Wanting the next video.
**Instrumentation:** Brighter synth, slight lift in energy, melodic hook if possible
**Volume in mix:** Slowly rise from 20% to 35% under the final "Subscribe" lines

**Key moment:** Under "Subscribe now. Hit the bell." — music should be at its warmest and most memorable. This is the earworm moment.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic uplifting conclusion`
- `lo-fi tech outro warm`
- `ambient intelligent electronic optimistic`

## SHORT-FORM VIDEO (65 sec) — Single Track

**Track duration needed:** 65–75 sec
**Energy level:** 7.5/10 — higher energy than long-form to match Reels/Shorts pacing
**BPM:** 105–112
**Mood:** High focus, forward momentum, smart and punchy
**Instrumentation:** Lo-fi tech beat with a clear pulse, short intro, no slow buildup

**Specific requirement:** Track must have energy from the first second — no slow intro. This is Reels, not YouTube.

**Volume in mix:** 25–30% throughout, rise to 35% on CTA

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `lo-fi tech punchy short`
- `electronic focused reels background`
- `minimal tech beat energetic`

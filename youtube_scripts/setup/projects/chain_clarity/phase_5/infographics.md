## CARD 01 — REAL-WORLD BLOCKCHAIN MAP

**Concept:** A visual map of blockchain applications across industries — not crypto/finance. Shows breadth of real use cases.

**Layout:** Central hexagonal logo (Chain Clarity brand mark) surrounded by 6 industry sectors in a radial layout, each with an icon and use case.

| Sector | Icon | Use Case |
|--------|------|---------|
| Supply Chain | 🚢 Container ship | Track shipments — immutable record |
| Healthcare | 🏥 Cross | Patient record ownership, drug tracing |
| Identity | 🪪 ID card | Self-sovereign credentials for 1.1B |
| Voting | 🗳️ Ballot | Auditable, tamper-proof elections |
| Real Estate | 🏠 House | Land registry, instant title transfer |
| Gaming | 🎮 Controller | True item ownership beyond the game |

**Color rules:**
- Background: #0A0E1A
- Central hex: Brand SVG (teal/navy/gold)
- Sector icons: alternating #00D4AA and #F5A623 strokes
- Labels: #FFFFFF (sector names), #8B9BB4 (use case descriptions)
- Connecting lines: #8B9BB4 at 25% opacity

**Animation sequence:**
1. Background fades in (0.2s)
2. Logo slides from top (0.3s, delay 0.3s)
3. Headline types in word by word (delay 0.6s)
4. Central hex bounces in (0.4s, delay 1.2s)
5. Sectors slide in radially one by one (0.25s each, 0.15s stagger, start 1.6s)
6. Stat bar bounces in: "6 industries. One underlying technology." (delay 2.8s)

**Stat:** "Supply chain · Identity · Health · Voting · Real estate · Gaming"
**CTA strip:** "Which use case surprised you most?"

## CARD 02 — NFT ACCURACY CARD

**Concept:** A simple, clean comparison that corrects the common misconception about NFTs. Two columns: what people think vs what NFTs actually are.

**Layout:** Two-column split with a clear divider.

| Left — The Noise | Right — The Signal |
|------------------|-------------------|
| "Overpriced JPEGs" | Ownership records |
| Speculation | Verifiable proof |
| Only for collectors | Tickets · Items · Land |
| Died in 2022 | Infrastructure continues |

**Bottom section:** Three real examples as pill cards:
1. NBA Top Shot: sports moment ownership (#00D4AA)
2. Concert tickets with auto-royalties (#F5A623)
3. Georgia land registry (#7B5CF0)

**Color rules:**
- Background: #0A0E1A
- Left column (noise): #8B9BB4 text, subtle strikethrough on key phrases
- Right column (signal): #FFFFFF text, #00D4AA accents
- Divider: vertical #8B9BB4 line, 30% opacity
- Example pills: each with respective accent color border

**Animation sequence:**
1. Background fades in (0.2s)
2. Logo slides from top (0.3s, delay 0.3s)
3. Headline types in (delay 0.6s)
4. Left column slides in from left (0.4s, delay 1.4s)

**Stat:** "NFT = ownership record. The tech outlived the bubble."

## CARD 03 — LAYER 2 EXPLAINER

**Concept:** A simple visual explaining how Layer 2 networks work using the bar tab analogy.

**Layout:** Three-step flow diagram, top to bottom.

| Step | Visual | Label |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Multiple drink icons → bar tab | "Off-chain: fast & cheap transactions" |
| 2 | Single receipt | "Periodic settlement batch" |
| 3 | Ethereum base layer block | "On-chain: one settlement per batch" |

**Right side stats panel (vertical):**
- Ethereum L1: 15 tx/s · ~$5 fee
- Polygon L2: 7,000 tx/s · $0.001 fee
- Lightning BTC: 1M tx/s · near zero

**Color rules:**
- Background: #0A0E1A
- Step numbers: #F5A623
- Flow arrows: #00D4AA
- L1 block: #7B5CF0 border (Ethereum violet)
- L2 blocks: #00D4AA border
- Stats: #FFFFFF values, #8B9BB4 labels

**Animation sequence:**
1. Background fades in (0.2s)
2. Logo slides from top (0.3s, delay 0.3s)
3. Headline types in (delay 0.6s)
4. Step 1 slides up (0.35s, delay 1.2s)
5. Arrow draws down (CSS, delay 1.55s)
6. Step 2 slides up (0.35s, delay 1.7s)
7. Arrow draws down (CSS, delay 2.05s)
8. Step 3 slides up (0.35s, delay 2.2s)
9. Stats panel slides in from right (0.4s, delay 2.5s)
10. Stat bar bounces in: "Off-chain speed. On-chain security." (delay 2.9s)

**Stat:** "Off-chain speed. On-chain security."
**CTA strip:** "This is how blockchain reaches billions of users."

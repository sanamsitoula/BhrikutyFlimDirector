# Voiceover Brief — Phase 5
## Chain Clarity · Blockchain Beyond Crypto

**Target duration:** 12 minutes (720 seconds)
**Delivery style:** Thoughtful, wide-lens perspective. A senior technologist reflecting on a technology's real trajectory. Calm confidence. This is the closing chapter — the tone should carry completion and meaning, not just information.

---

## PACING GUIDE

### Hook (0:00–0:12) — 70 words
- **Pace:** Measured, factual. Each stat is a statement, not a dramatic reveal.
- **Emphasis:** "$14 billion in cargo" — let the number land.
- **Emphasis:** "No token required." — a short beat of silence after. This is the reframe.
- **Close:** "Blockchain is infrastructure." — firm. Authoritative.

### Section 1 — Real Problem (0:12–2:30) — ~220 words
- **Pace:** Deliberate. Build the problem clearly before offering the solution.
- **Emphasis:** "Whose record is correct?" — slight rise in pitch. A genuine question.
- **Emphasis:** "One shared ledger that nobody owns." — slow. Let each word land.
- **Tone:** Intellectual curiosity. We are reasoning through a real design problem.

### Section 2 — Supply Chain (2:30–4:30) — ~200 words
- **Pace:** Story-like. The Walmart mango story is a narrative — give it rhythm.
- **Emphasis:** "Six days, eighteen hours, twenty-six minutes." — deliberate, each unit spoken fully.
- **Emphasis:** "2.2 seconds." — shorter pause. The contrast does the work.
- **Tone:** Grounded. These are facts, not claims.

### Section 3 — Digital Identity (4:30–6:30) — ~220 words
- **Pace:** Slightly slower on the 1.1 billion statistic. Weight.
- **Emphasis:** "1.1 billion people in the world have no legal identity." — serious, still.
- **How it works section:** Slightly faster, methodical — like walking through a diagram.
- **Estonia/UN references:** Grounded, factual. Real examples anchor the concept.

### Section 4 — NFTs (6:30–8:30) — ~230 words
- **Pace:** Start slower — reclaim the term from its noisy past.
- **Emphasis:** "That was noise. Here is the signal." — clear pivot. Slight emphasis on "signal."
- **Examples:** Concert tickets, game items, land records — each delivered crisply. Not rushed.
- **Tone:** Corrective but not combative. We are clarifying, not debunking.

### Section 5 — Layer 2 (8:30–10:30) — ~220 words
- **Pace:** Technical but accessible. The bar tab analogy deserves a slight warmth.
- **Emphasis:** "15 transactions per second. Visa: 24,000." — contrast. Let both numbers land.
- **Bar tab analogy:** Conversational delivery. This is the moment of levity.
- **Tone:** Forward-looking. We are building toward the resolution of a constraint.

### Close (10:30–12:00) — ~100 words
- **Pace:** Reflective. This is the chapter close — not a wrap-up, a completion.
- **Emphasis:** "The speculation was loud. The infrastructure is quieter." — measured, true.
- **Thank you close:** Warm. Genuine. No forced enthusiasm.
- **Tone:** A mentor handing over a complete mental model. Earned.

## TECHNICAL SPECS

- **Sample rate:** 44.1 kHz stereo (or 24 kHz mono for Qwen3-TTS)
- **Format:** WAV
- **Target loudness:** –16 LUFS integrated (YouTube standard)
- **Silence padding:** 0.3s before first word, 0.5s after last word

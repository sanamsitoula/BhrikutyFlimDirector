# Music Brief — Phase 4
## Chain Clarity · Security & Self-Custody

---

## OVERALL MOOD

Focused vigilance. Like a security professional walking through a system audit.
Serious, grounded, intelligent — not threatening or paranoid.
The listener should feel empowered and capable, not scared.
Think: a clean room at 2am, concentrated work, a methodical mind at full attention.

---

## SECTION BREAKDOWNS

### Intro / Hook (0:00–0:12)
- **Mood:** Tense, quiet attention. Like the moment before something important.
- **Instrumentation:** Sparse. Single low pad, maybe a single high note that resolves.
- **BPM:** 88–96
- **Dynamics:** Low. Set the tone without overwhelming the opening stat delivery.

### Sections 1–2 — Keys & Wallets (0:12–4:00)
- **Mood:** Informed, measured. Educational but slightly serious.
- **Instrumentation:** Lo-fi electronic. Subtle arpeggios in background. Minimal percussion.
- **BPM:** 92–100
- **Dynamics:** Consistent low level under narration.

### Section 3 — Seed Phrase (4:00–6:00)
- **Mood:** Weight. Gravity. This is the most important section.
- **Instrumentation:** Strip back to a single pad. Almost ambient. Let the words carry.
- **BPM:** 88–92
- **Dynamics:** Slightly lower than previous section. More space.

### Section 4 — Attack Vectors (6:00–9:00)
- **Mood:** Alert but controlled. Like a security briefing.
- **Instrumentation:** Slightly more rhythmic. Subtle kick or click underneath. Still lo-fi.
- **BPM:** 96–104
- **Dynamics:** Slight raise from Section 3. Forward momentum.

### Section 5 — Checklist + Close (9:00–11:00)
- **Mood:** Methodical confidence. Building toward resolution.
- **Instrumentation:** Return to arpeggio motif from Sections 1–2. Feels like completion.
- **BPM:** 96–100
- **Dynamics:** Gradual fade beginning at 10:30 to allow clean outro.

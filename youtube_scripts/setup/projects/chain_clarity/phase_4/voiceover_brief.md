# Voiceover Brief — Phase 4
## Chain Clarity · Security & Self-Custody

**Target duration:** 11 minutes (660 seconds)
**Delivery style:** Senior security engineer — measured, precise, calm authority. No alarm or panic. Serious without being alarmist. Like a trusted mentor walking through a checklist before a mission.

---

## PACING GUIDE

### Hook (0:00–0:12) — 72 words
- **Pace:** Deliberate. Each stat lands like a fact, not a shock.
- **Emphasis:** "not a beginner" — pause after. "And they still clicked the wrong link." — slight drop in pace, serious tone.
- **Breath:** Natural pause after each sentence. No rushing.

### Section 1 — Keys & Custody (0:12–2:00) — ~200 words
- **Pace:** Measured. Textbook clarity.
- **Emphasis:** "Not your keys, not your coins." — slow, one word at a time, full stop after each.
- **Emphasis:** "Full stop." — pause before and after. Two words. Weight.
- **Tone shift:** On "If the exchange fails — like FTX" — slightly lower pitch, grounded.

### Section 2 — Hot vs Cold (2:00–4:00) — ~220 words
- **Pace:** Slightly faster on the wallet list (MetaMask, Phantom, Trust Wallet — quick enumeration).
- **Emphasis:** "The device itself is not the asset. The seed phrase is the asset." — slow. Clear. This is the key insight.
- **Analogy delivery:** Coffee money / safe deposit box — warm, conversational. Slight smile in voice.

### Section 3 — Seed Phrase (4:00–6:00) — ~210 words
- **Pace:** Steady throughout.
- **Emphasis:** Seed phrase storage list (photo, note app, cloud, email) — each item punchy and final. Like closing doors.
- **Emphasis:** "If someone finds your seed phrase — they own your funds. Permanently." — pause before "Permanently." Let it sit.
- **Metal backup:** Slight lift in energy. This is the answer. Confident.

### Section 4 — Attack Vectors (6:00–9:00) — ~320 words
- **Pace:** Introduce each attack with a slightly slower opening line. Speed up the explanation. Slow again on the rule.
- **Emphasis:** "Never" — each use of the word never is spoken with full weight. Not raised voice. Finality.
- **Enumeration:** "Attack one... Attack two..." — clear verbal markers, consistent cadence, slight beat pause between each.
- **Rule delivery:** Each rule stated at the end of each attack section — slower, deliberate. These are the takeaways.

### Section 5 — Checklist (9:00–10:30) — ~150 words
- **Pace:** Steady, methodical. Checklist energy. Like reading items off a list with purpose.
- **Numbers:** "One... Two... Three..." — clear, unhurried. Each item complete before moving.
- **Close:** "Security is not a product you buy. It is a practice you build." — slow, measured, strong ending.

### Close (10:30–11:00) — ~70 words
- **Pace:** Thoughtful. Slight warmth on "That knowledge is Chain Clarity Phase 4."
- **Subscribe CTA:** Natural, not forced. Conversational energy.

## TECHNICAL SPECS

- **Sample rate:** 44.1 kHz stereo (or 24 kHz mono for Qwen3-TTS)
- **Format:** WAV
- **Target loudness:** –16 LUFS integrated (YouTube standard)
- **Silence padding:** 0.3s before first word, 0.5s after last word
- **Segment splits:** Split at section markers for ffmpeg assembly

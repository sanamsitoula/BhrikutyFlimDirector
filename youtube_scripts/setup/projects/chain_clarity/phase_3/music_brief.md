# Music Brief — Phase 3
## Chain Clarity · "DeFi Explained — How Finance Works Without Banks"

**Brand sound identity:** Electronic ambient / lo-fi tech · 88–112 BPM · focused, intelligent, grounded
**Phase 3 tone:** More mechanical and concrete than Phases 1–2. This is about systems, mechanisms, and real stakes. Music should feel like watching a machine operate — precise, relentless, occasionally tense. The risks section needs gravity without panic.

---

## LONG-FORM VIDEO (14 min)

---

### HOOK (0:00–0:15)

**Energy:** 7.5/10 — contrast and disruption
**BPM:** 108–112
**Mood:** The gap between old finance and new finance. Bank → code. Formal → automatic.
**Instrumentation:** Brief institutional piano chord (cold, formal), then cut to clean electronic pulse as "In 2024" begins. The contrast is the point.
**Volume in mix:** 30% — slightly higher than Phase 2 hook; this one opens with more certainty
**Dynamics:** Start at 20%, cut on "In 2024, you can borrow money from a protocol" → jump to 35%

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic pulse contrast institutional`
- `minimal tech disruption ambient`
- `clean electronic pivot underscore`

---

### SECTION 1 — SMART CONTRACTS (0:15–2:30)

**Energy:** 6/10 — mechanical precision, building understanding
**BPM:** 96–102
**Mood:** The feeling of watching a machine work exactly as designed. Clockwork logic. IF/THEN clarity.
**Instrumentation:** Steady rhythmic pulse, sparse arpeggio, subtle bass. No melody yet — the music should feel like a process running.
**Volume in mix:** 15–18%

**Key moment:** When "The code is the bank." is delivered — music does a very brief filter close (100ms) then reopens. The silence around the line is intentional. Match the weight.

**Key moment:** When the IF/THEN lending example begins — music can add a very subtle second layer (soft pad) to signal complexity building. Remove it again when the analogy ("pawn shop") arrives.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `mechanical electronic process ambient`
- `lo-fi tech systematic focused`
- `electronic clockwork minimal underscore`

---

### SECTION 2 — DEFI LENDING (2:30–5:00)

**Energy:** 6.5/10 — methodical, slightly more momentum than Section 1
**BPM:** 98–104
**Mood:** A system at work. Money moving. Rules executing. There is efficiency here, and the music should reflect that.
**Instrumentation:** Slightly fuller rhythmic layer than Section 1. You can add a gentle bassline. Still lo-fi, still controlled. No drama.
**Volume in mix:** 18–20%

**Key moment:** "Automatic. Instant. Unstoppable." — music should have a brief accent hit on each of the three words (very subtle — a hi-hat tap or a filter bloom). Then settle.

**Key moment:** As the pawn shop analogy plays — music goes slightly warmer. Analog-leaning. Match the human-scale metaphor.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `lo-fi electronic efficiency systematic`
- `ambient tech mechanism rhythmic`
- `focused electronic flow underscore`

---

### SECTION 3 — AMMS AND LIQUIDITY POOLS (5:00–7:30)

**Energy:** 6/10 — mathematical, almost meditative
**BPM:** 96–102
**Mood:** The constant product formula. x times y equals k. The music should feel like a balance being maintained — a weight on each side of a scale, always equal. Almost hypnotic.
**Instrumentation:** Two-voice arrangement works well here — a low note and a high note alternating, like two pools balancing. Sparse percussion. No busy layers.
**Volume in mix:** 15–18%

**Key moment:** As "No one sets the price. The math does." lands — brief musical resolution. A chord landing or a filter opening. This is the section's conceptual payoff.

**Note:** This is the most abstract section of Phase 3. The music must not compete with the math explanation. Simpler is better here.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `mathematical ambient electronic minimal`
- `lo-fi balance two-voice meditation`
- `electronic meditative focused sparse`

---

### SECTION 4 — YIELD AND RISK (7:30–9:30)

**Energy:** 5.5/10 — cautious, measured, honest
**BPM:** 92–98
**Mood:** Not fear. Not panic. Honesty. The music should feel like a safety briefing from someone who genuinely knows the risks. Grounded, slightly serious.
**Instrumentation:** Pull back from Section 3. Fewer elements. Soft pad, gentle pulse. The sparseness signals seriousness.
**Volume in mix:** 12–15%

**Key moment:** "If someone cannot explain what service produces the yield — walk away." — brief musical pause on "walk away." A single beat of silence. Then music resumes.

**Key moment:** When impermanent loss is explained — music stays quiet. This is complex math. Do not compete.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `lo-fi honest cautious ambient`
- `electronic measured serious underscore`
- `minimal ambient warning focused`

---

### SECTION 5 — STABLECOINS (9:30–11:30)

**Energy:** 5.5/10 — informational, slightly warmer toward the end
**BPM:** 94–100
**Mood:** Structure and taxonomy. Explaining the three types, building toward the DAI (crypto-backed) conclusion which feels most aligned with DeFi's ethos.
**Instrumentation:** Clean pad, gentle rhythm. Slightly warmer tones than Section 4.
**Volume in mix:** 15%

**Key moment:** "Within four days, 40 billion dollars in value was destroyed." — music drops to near-silence for this line. Absolute minimum volume. Let the number breathe.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic ambient informational clean`
- `lo-fi structured focused warm`

---

### SECTION 6 — RISKS (11:30–13:15)

**Energy:** 5/10 — deliberate, grounded, non-alarmist
**BPM:** 90–96
**Mood:** A clear-eyed safety briefing. Not a horror show. The music should feel like the narrator trusts the listener to handle the truth.
**Instrumentation:** Minimal — softest of the video. Sparse pad, almost no percussion. The simplicity mirrors the directness of the content.
**Volume in mix:** 10–12%

**Note:** Do NOT let music add drama to the risk section. This section is most effective when the words carry the weight alone. Music is nearly inaudible here.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `minimal ambient honest direct`
- `lo-fi sparse background underscore`

---

### CTA (13:15–14:00)

**Energy:** 7/10 — resolved, confident, forward
**BPM:** 106–112
**Mood:** "DeFi is not magic. It is math and code." — satisfaction, earned knowledge, forward momentum toward Phase 4.
**Instrumentation:** Return to warmer tones. Melodic hook if available. More presence than any section since Section 2.
**Volume in mix:** Fade up from 22% to 38% under final subscribe lines

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic ambient resolved confident`
- `lo-fi tech outro satisfaction forward`
- `intelligent electronic warm conclusion`

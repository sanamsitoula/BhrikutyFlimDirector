# Music Brief — Phase 2
## Chain Clarity · "Bitcoin vs Ethereum — What's Actually Different"

**Brand sound identity:** Electronic ambient / lo-fi tech · 88–112 BPM · focused, intelligent, grounded
**Phase 2 tone shift:** Slightly more confident and revelatory than Phase 1. We're building on established foundations. Music should feel like momentum, not just explanation.

---

## LONG-FORM VIDEO (14 min)

---

### HOOK (0:00–0:15)

**Energy:** 8/10 — weight and wonder together
**BPM:** 105–110
**Mood:** The feeling of realizing a rule you assumed was flexible is actually permanent
**Instrumentation:** Deep bass pulse, sparse high piano note, slow filter sweep
**Dynamics:** Start at 25%, swell to 40% under "21 million. No more. Ever."
**Volume in mix:** 30% — slightly higher than Phase 1 hook to match the bolder opening

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `cinematic tech weight minimal`
- `electronic ambient revelation`
- `sparse piano deep bass electronic`

---

### SECTION 1 — BITCOIN'S DIFFERENCE (0:15–2:30)

**Energy:** 5/10 — intellectual curiosity, slightly contrarian
**BPM:** 92–96
**Mood:** Reframing something the viewer thought they understood
**Instrumentation:** Minimal lo-fi, soft pad, very occasional hi-hat
**Volume in mix:** 15%

**Transition note:** When "The code IS the policy" is delivered, music can have a very brief filter close (50ms) then reopen — a micro-punctuation under the biggest line of the section.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `lo-fi electronic intellectual`
- `ambient reframe focused`

---

### SECTION 2 — PROOF OF WORK (2:30–5:00)

**Energy:** 6/10 — mechanical precision, building process
**BPM:** 96–102
**Mood:** The relentless forward march of computation. Mathematical inevitability.
**Instrumentation:** Steady rhythmic pulse, mechanical-feeling percussion (not trap — more like a clock), arpeggio
**Volume in mix:** 18%

**Key moment:** As the "combination lock" analogy begins, music should feel slightly more tactile — as if you can hear the effort. A subtle shift in the percussion layer is enough.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `mechanical ambient electronic lo-fi`
- `focused computation electronic underscore`
- `electronic pulse rhythmic minimal`

---

### SECTION 3 — BITCOIN'S SUPPLY (5:00–7:30)

**Energy:** 6.5/10 — building reverence as halvings count down
**BPM:** 98–105
**Mood:** The tick of a clock that started in 2009 and will run until 2140. Inevitability with a sense of scale.
**Instrumentation:** Slightly warmer pad, rhythmic pulse that could represent blocks being mined every 10 minutes
**Volume in mix:** 15–20%

**Key moment:** As the halving sequence plays (50 → 25 → 12.5...), the music should subtly build. When "Total supply: 21 million. Final." is delivered — brief musical pause or filter drop, then resume. Match the gravity of the line.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic ambient scale inevitable`
- `lo-fi focused warm rhythmic`
- `minimal electronic countdown ambient`

---

### SECTION 4 — ETHEREUM (7:30–9:30)

**Energy:** 7/10 — revelation and possibility
**BPM:** 100–108
**Mood:** Opening a door. The moment a single question changed the trajectory of an industry.
**Instrumentation:** Brighter synth lead (but still understated), more harmonic richness, slight uplift from prior sections
**Volume in mix:** 18–22%

**Key moment:** "What if we could run code without a server?" — music should shift here. A new section. More open sound. Like a new chapter starting. Even a gentle new pad layer entering would work.

**Note:** This is the highest-energy explanatory section. The music should feel like discovery, not just background.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic ambient discovery opening`
- `lo-fi tech new chapter uplift`
- `intelligent electronic brighter focused`

---

### SECTION 5 — PROOF OF STAKE (9:30–11:30)

**Energy:** 6/10 — analytical and resolved
**BPM:** 98–105
**Mood:** Methodical explanation of a system that replaced another. Analytical but with satisfaction — The Merge was a significant achievement.
**Instrumentation:** Cleaner, slightly more resolved harmonics than PoW section. The contrast should be audible.
**Volume in mix:** 15%

**Key moment:** "Ethereum's energy use dropped 99.9% after The Merge." — subtle lift in music here. Not dramatic, but present. Acknowledge the magnitude.

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic ambient analytical resolved`
- `lo-fi tech focused clean`

---

### SECTION 6 — WALLETS AND KEYS (11:30–13:15)

**Energy:** 5/10 — precise and practical
**BPM:** 92–98
**Mood:** Careful explanation of something consequential. The tone is: "this part actually matters for your safety."
**Instrumentation:** Back to minimal — soft pad, gentle pulse. The simplicity mirrors the simplicity of the three-layer concept.
**Volume in mix:** 12–15%

**Note:** Do not let music compete with the wallet/key explanations. This section has practical safety implications. Background only.

---

### CTA (13:15–14:00)

**Energy:** 7/10 — satisfying, forward-looking
**BPM:** 105–110
**Mood:** The feeling of having genuinely understood something complex. Momentum toward Phase 3.
**Instrumentation:** Return to warmer harmonics, slight melodic hook, more presence
**Volume in mix:** Fade up from 20% to 35% under final subscribe lines

**Royalty-free search terms:**
- `electronic ambient warm conclusion`
- `lo-fi tech satisfying forward`
- `intelligent electronic outro momentum`

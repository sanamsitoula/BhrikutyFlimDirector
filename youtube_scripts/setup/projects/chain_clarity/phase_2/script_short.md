SPOKEN:
"Your Bitcoin lives on the blockchain.
What the wallet holds is the key that proves you own it.

There are three layers.

ONE: the private key.
A 256-bit random number.
Whoever holds this owns the coins.
Lose it — gone forever.
Share it — gone immediately.

TWO: the public key.
Derived from the private key through one-way math.
You can go forward. You cannot go back.

THREE: your address.
Derived from the public key.
This is what you share. Like a mailbox number.

When you send crypto, you sign the transaction with your private key.
The signature proves ownership — without ever revealing the key.

The analogy:
Your address is your mailbox.
Your private key is the seal stamp.
Only you can authorize outgoing packages."
